## Your data stays yours

Swelog runs entirely on your own computer. There is no swelog account and no
swelog server, so your notes and credentials never reach us. Nothing leaves your
machine until you opt into an integration or summarization. See the
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy/) for what the tool stores, what it sends over
the network, and what happens to the notes you hand to a language model, and the
[Terms of Service](/legal/terms/) for the terms you agree to by using it.
